# clinstats: Clinical Research Statistics Toolkit

A compact set of statistical utilities that recur in clinical and
translational research: ROC summaries, odds ratios, univariable and
multivariable Cox and logistic regression tables, survival cut points,
resampling-based Cox screening, TCGA clinical extraction, and
expression-matrix cleaning. Curated colorectal cancer clinical datasets
assembled from public repositories are included, together with a Quarto
analysis report template.
